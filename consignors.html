Fees

Nomination & Commission

$1,500 + 9%

Nomination fee per head, plus 9% commission on the sale price.

$500 Optional

Full-page catalog ad, due at time of nomination. Designed in-house. Refunded with your nomination fee if the horse isn't selected. Non-refundable after December 1.

Requirements

Ownership & horse standards.

  • Horse papers must be in the consignor's name by sale day — no exceptions. No double transfers; original papers plus a signed transfer are due at check-in.
  • No grade horses accepted. No mares or stallions unless approved by sale staff prior to nomination.
  • Current (within 6 months) Coggins and health certificate to unload on-site required.
  • All horses must pass a standard vet exam at check-in and are guaranteed for 72 hours from the end of the sale (seller responsible for guarantees).
  • Radiographs are required, taken within 90 days of the sale and submitted by March 1, 2027, to horsesales@ranchorioaz.com.
  • Videos (Vimeo or YouTube only, no raw files) are optional for nomination but required for first-time consignors.
  • Photos must be professional and high-resolution (1–5 MB), well-groomed horse, no foreign objects in background, clean tack if saddled.

Please review the full Sale Terms & Conditions before nominating your horse.
