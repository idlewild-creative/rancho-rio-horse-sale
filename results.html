Sale History, 2015–2026

A Decade of Growth. From a $15,541 sale average in 2018 to $60,152 in 2026 — the market for elite rope horses has never stopped climbing at Rancho Rio. That rise tracks the effort behind it: our returning consignors who ride, rope, haul, and train from sunup to sundown, year after year, to bring a truly finished horse to the ring. It's why we celebrate the modern-day rope horse and this industry — but most of all, the people and families who make it what it is.
