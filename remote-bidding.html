Bidding Online

Get your buyer number.

  • Watching requires no account, but bidding does — you'll need a validated buyer number to place bids.
  • To get one, go to the sale page on CCI.live and select “Get Buyer Number.” Bidder numbers become available 5 business days before the sale.
  • A $1,000 credit card hold is required to obtain a remote bidding number.
  • Works on cellular devices, laptops, desktops, and tablets. A strong, stationary internet connection is essential — if you're driving and bidding, expect some service issues.
  • Real-time asking prices display on screen. If things look laggy, refreshing your browser usually clears it up.
