Horse Warranty Summary

Except as otherwise announced by the auctioneer, the consignor warrants that: title of the horse is free and clear; the animal is sound of eyes, wind, and limb; the animal is not a cribber, windsucker, stall weaver, or parrot mouthed; the animal has not been nerved, does not have navicular disease, and is not foundered; and the sex or condition as a gelding or ridgeling is as described in the sale catalog or announced at time of sale. If any of these conditions are announced at time of sale, the consignor is held harmless for that condition. Horses are guaranteed for 72 hours from the end of the sale, with the seller responsible for all guarantees.
