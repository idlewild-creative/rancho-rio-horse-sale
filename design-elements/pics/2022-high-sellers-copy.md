# 2022 High Sellers — source copy (pulled from ranchorioaz.com/high-sellers-2022/)

Source page intro: The Horse Sale at Rancho Rio presented by Gold Buckle Beer took place Friday, March 11, in Wickenburg, Arizona, in conjunction with the National Team Roping (NTR) National Finals VIII. The 65 consignments at the eighth annual sale drew spectators and buyers from all over the world and saw $2.3 million in team roping horse power go through the sale ring. With 48 horses sold, the overall sale average was $48,656.

## Lot 31: Sportin Roscoe — $200,000 (image: 2022_lot31_sportin-roscoe_200k.jpg)
Lot 31, Sportin Roscoe, was the high seller when the gavel dropped at $200,000. The 2013 chestnut gelding called "South Point" was consigned by New Mexico's Brooke and Rodey Wilson of Wilson Cattle Company and purchased by Shane O'Hotto, Fort Lupton, Colorado. The ranch-raised gelding sired by Sportin High Brow was bred by Jim Rounsaville, Olney, Texas. "He's a once-in-a-lifetime kind of horse," said Brooke, who has campaigned South Point as her No. 1 mount for several years. "I don't think I'll ever get to own another one as special as he is. I count every day with him as a blessing. A good horse is a good horse—they make themselves. His first jackpot I was high call, and the rest is history. He's a natural, just a good horse."
